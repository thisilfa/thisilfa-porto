# Home Page visual guide:
What the audience will see once they open my page?
--> my vision is to put petal animation. A few petals drifting around my headshot or the empty spaces on the homepace:
- Pale dusty pink and muted blush petals.
- Different petal sizes.
- Slow, slightly randomized falling motion.
- Gentle horizontal drifting.
- Some petals rotate as they fall.
- Low opacity so they don't distract from your content.

#### petal animation guide:
Setting	Recommendation
Petal count	8–12 visible
Color	#D8A7AD
Opacity	0.3–0.55
Duration	14–24 seconds
Interaction	Never block clicks
Mobile	Reduce to 4–6 petals
Accessibility	Respect reduced-motion settings

##### the petals appear primarily on the homepage and disappear when someone enters my technical case studies.
